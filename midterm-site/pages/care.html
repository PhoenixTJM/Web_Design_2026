02 / A gentle routine

Care for your
own kind of crown.

Afro-textured hair is wonderfully diverse. A good routine starts with patience, gentle handling, and noticing what works for you.

01

Cleanse gently

Use a cleanser that feels comfortable for your scalp. Work in sections if that makes washing easier, and avoid rough scrubbing along the hair lengths.

02

Add moisture

Conditioning can help hair feel softer and easier to detangle. Try applying products to damp hair and adjust how much you use based on how your hair responds.

03

Detangle with patience

Work gently, using fingers or a wide-tooth comb, starting at the ends and moving upward. Conditioner or a detangling product may help reduce pulling.

04

Protect your ends

Low-tension styles and a satin or silk scarf or pillowcase can help limit friction. If a style hurts or pulls, loosen it—comfort matters.

No one-size-fits-all

Hair density, curl pattern, scalp needs, climate, and personal preference all make a difference. Use these ideas as a starting point, not strict rules.
