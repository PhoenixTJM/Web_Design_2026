03 / Wear it your way

There’s no wrong
way to take up space.

Your afro can change with your mood, your plans, or simply the day. Try a look because it feels good to you—not because it follows a rule.

A few ideas

Start with what feels like you.

  • The rounded shapePick gently for a fuller, softly shaped silhouette.
  • Side partShift your part to change the shape without changing your texture.
  • Small detailsAdd a headband, scarf, clip, or a few cuffs to make it personal.
  • Protective looksTry twists, braids, or a puff when you want a different routine.

Keep styles comfortable and low-tension. Your scalp should never have to hurt for a look.

Make it yours

Volume is not a requirement.
Neither is a particular shape.
