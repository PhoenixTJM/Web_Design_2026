About SUNY New Paltz

The State University of New York at New Paltz is a public university located in New Paltz, New York. The university offers students a combination of strong academics, creative opportunities, campus activities, and access to the natural beauty of the Hudson Valley.

Arts & Design

Located in the Hudson Valley

SUNY New Paltz is located in the scenic Hudson Valley, surrounded by mountains, trails, and historic communities. Its location provides students with opportunities to explore both campus life and the outdoors.
