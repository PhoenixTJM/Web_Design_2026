About SUNY New Paltz

SUNY New Paltz is a public university located in New Paltz, New York. The university offers a wide range of undergraduate and graduate programs while providing students with opportunities to learn, create, and become involved in their community.

Academics

Computer Science

Develop programming, problem-solving, and technical skills for careers in the technology industry.

Arts & Design

Explore creative fields including art, design, digital media, and other visual disciplines.

Business

Build skills in management, entrepreneurship, finance, marketing, and organizational leadership.

Campus Life

Student Organizations

Get involved with clubs, organizations, events, and activities throughout campus.

Hudson Valley

Experience the natural beauty of the Hudson Valley and the surrounding Catskill Mountains.

Campus Community

Meet new people, collaborate with classmates, and build connections that last beyond college.
